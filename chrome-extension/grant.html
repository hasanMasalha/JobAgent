Let JobAgent fill in Greenhouse applications

To apply for you, the extension needs access to Greenhouse's pages (greenhouse.io). Chrome will ask you to allow it. With it, the extension:

  • opens the application form only for jobs you click Apply on;
  • fills it in with your details, your CV and the answers you saved;
  • stops without submitting when a required question has no answer from you.

It doesn't read other Greenhouse pages. You can remove the access in Chrome's extension settings at any time.
